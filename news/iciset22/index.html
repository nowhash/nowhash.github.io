Received the Best Paper Award at ICISET 2022

By Abu Nowhash Chowdhury · April 1, 2022 · 2 min read
  • NLP
  • ICISET 2022
  • IEEE
  • International Islamic University Chittagong

I am happy to share that I received the Best Paper Award at the 3rd International Conference on Innovations in Science, Engineering, and Technology (ICISET 2022).

The award-winning paper, titled “Exploiting Diverse Contextual Features through Transformers for Detecting Informative Tweets,” was co-authored by Abu Nowhash Chowdhury, Shawon Guha, Nurul Amin, and Shahidul Islam Khan.

Our research focused on identifying informative tweets and distinguishing them from uninformative content during critical events such as the COVID-19 pandemic. We explored different pre-trained transformer models, including BERT, CT-BERT, BERTweet, RoBERTa, and XLM-RoBERTa, to capture diverse contextual features and improve informative tweet detection.

I am thankful to our supervisor, Dr. Shahidul Islam Khan, for his valuable guidance, continuous support, and encouragement throughout this research. His advice and insights played an important role in completing this work.

The conference was organized by the International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC) and technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Bangladesh Section.

Receiving this award was an important milestone in my research journey and an encouraging recognition of our team’s work.

Publication Details

  • Paper: Exploiting Diverse Contextual Features through Transformers for Detecting Informative Tweets
  • Authors: Abu Nowhash Chowdhury, Shawon Guha, Nurul Amin, and Shahidul Islam Khan
  • Conference: 3rd International Conference on Innovations in Science, Engineering, and Technology (ICISET 2022)
  • Pages: 350–355
  • DOI: 10.1109/ICISET54810.2022.9775891
