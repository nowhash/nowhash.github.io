Crafting a Research Paper: Insights from My Session at IIUC DSRG

By Abu Nowhash Chowdhury · January 28, 2023 · 1 min read
  • Research
  • Research Paper
  • Research Talk
  • IIUC Data Science and Research Group, Data Science

On 28th January 2023, I conducted a research session on Beginning of a Researcher. This session was organized by IIUC Data Science Research Group

In this session, I’ve discussed several fundamental components that a CS research paper must have. I’ve demonstrated how to organize those components properly in a research paper.

Additionally, I also covered important guidelines for referencing and citation, as well as tips for making the paper visually appealing and easy to read. Overall, it was a productive session and I hope the attendees found it helpful in their research paper writing journey.
