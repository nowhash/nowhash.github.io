About

Abu Nowhash Chowdhury

アブ・ノウハショ・チョウドリ

Laboratory
Text Information Processing and Informatics (TIP)
Institution
Kitami Institute of Technology, Japan

Abu Nowhash Chowdhury received his B.Sc. (Engg.) in Computer Science and Engineering from the International Islamic University Chittagong, Bangladesh, in 2022. He served as a faculty member in the Department of Computer Science at the Asian University for Women (AUW), and is currently pursuing his graduate studies at the Text Information Processing and Informatics Laboratory (TIP), Kitami Institute of Technology, Japan.

His research interests include opinion mining from informal social media data, measuring the informativeness of user-generated text, deep learning, computational linguistics, natural language processing (NLP), and multimodal data analysis. One of his research articles received the Best Paper Award at the 3rd International Conference on Innovations in Science, Engineering, and Technology (ICISET), organized by IEEE, Bangladesh.

He is deeply interested in learning and teaching programming languages and is committed to making meaningful contributions to education, teaching, and research, with a particular focus on supporting young students in developing an interest in learning and research.
