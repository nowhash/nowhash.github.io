Language Acquisition and Understanding (LAU) Research Meeting 2026

By Abu Nowhash Chowdhury October 16, 2026 Posted in Conference
Tags: NLP LAU Hokkaido University of Science and Technology Mount Teine

Recently, I participated in the 16th Language Acquisition and Understanding (LAU) Research Meeting 2026, organized by the Hokkaido NLP Group and held at Hokkaido University of Science in Sapporo, Japan, on September 26–27.

Presenting My Research

At the meeting, I presented my research titled “Does Explicitly Providing Factual Context Influence a Vision-Language Model’s Interpretation of Propagandistic Memes?”

In this study, I investigated whether providing explicit factual context can influence how vision-language models interpret propagandistic memes. More specifically, I explored how different types of contextual information affect the interpretation of multimodal content.

Reconnecting with a Friend and Exploring Hokkaido

Besides the research meeting, I also had the opportunity to explore Sapporo and Teine. The trip was not just about research; I also had some wonderful personal experiences along the way!

One of the best parts of the trip was meeting my old friend Galib after around four years. It was really nice to see him again and catch up after such a long time.

I also explored several places in Sapporo. I visited the Sapporo Factory area, which is known for its distinctive glass atrium, and enjoyed walking around the shopping streets at night. The city had a lively atmosphere after dark, which made the experience even more enjoyable.

Another highlight was visiting Asahiyama Memorial Park, where I enjoyed the beautiful views of Sapporo. I also got the chance to explore the area around Mount Teine. And, of course, I tried Japan’s famous Wagyu ramen! It was nice to take a break from research, enjoy some good food, and explore more of Hokkaido.

Overall, it was a wonderful experience combining research, reconnecting with an old friend, trying new food, and exploring different places in Hokkaido. I am glad I had the opportunity to present my work at the LAU Research Meeting 2026 and make some great memories along the way.

Looking forward to more research opportunities and experiences like this in my academic journey!
